📹 Help Us Understand Your Video Habits

This survey takes about 5 minutes. Your insights will directly shape features that save you time and reduce the overwhelm of keeping up with all that content.

We're building Leka — a tool to extract the value from long videos and turn it into useful knowledge in minutes instead of hours.

Leka watches the video for you and removes the boring parts. A 60-minute video becomes a 5–10-minute summary you can read or listen to.

Want to know more? Ask Leka questions in the Chat to get answers from the video or follow timestamps to jump to the exact moment.

You can also turn any video into ready content: LinkedIn posts or short video scripts.

Spend less time watching. Get the value faster.

Important: When answering, please think only about YouTube and educational videos — not Instagram or TikTok.
